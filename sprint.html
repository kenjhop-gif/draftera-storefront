Staff training

How it works

  1. Week 1: find the task. We pick the work that costs the most time and is safest to start with. I meet the people who do it.
  2. Week 2: build and test. I build the workflow with your staff, using tools you already have where possible. We test it on real work.
  3. Week 3: train and measure. Staff learn to use it. We set the guardrails. I deliver the time-saved report.

An example of the kind of task we might tackle

A program manager spends two days each quarter pulling notes into a funder report. A Sprint could build a workflow that produces a first draft from those notes. The manager then reviews and edits it instead of starting from a blank page.

Your data stays protected

Client, donor and staff personal information never goes into outside AI tools without your written agreement. We decide together what data each workflow can use.
