01

Who it is for

  • Your staff already use AI tools, but informally and unevenly.
  • You have one or two tasks that eat hours every week or month.
  • You want results you can show your board, not another strategy document.

02

What you get

  • 1–2 working workflows,

    built around tasks your team already does.

  • Staff training,

    so the people using the workflow understand it and trust it.

  • Guardrails,

    in plain language: what staff can use AI for, what stays out of AI tools, and who checks the output.

  • A time-saved report,

    measuring hours before and after, and what that means in dollars.

03

How it works

  1. Week 1

    Find the task.

    We pick the work that costs the most time and is safest to start with. I meet the people who do it.

  2. Week 2

    Build and test.

    I build the workflow with your staff, using tools you already have where possible. We test it on real work.

  3. Week 3

    Train and measure.

    Staff learn to use it. We set the guardrails. I deliver the time-saved report.

04

An example of the kind of task we might tackle

Illustrative example

A program manager spends two days each quarter pulling notes into a funder report. A Quick Win Sprint could build a workflow that produces a first draft from those notes. The manager then reviews and edits it instead of starting from a blank page.

05

Your data stays protected

Client, donor and staff personal information never goes into outside AI tools without your written agreement. We decide together what data each workflow can use.
