Partner work

For partners

Working with networks, funders and sector bodies

Draftera helps nonprofits and social enterprises govern and use AI well. Alongside its work with individual organizations, Draftera works with the bodies that support them.

01

The need

Imagine Canada’s 2026 research found that 80% of Canadian nonprofits use AI, but only 10% have a formal AI policy. The main barriers are uncertainty and limited hands-on experience, not money. Canada already has national standards that address this, yet they are not easy for a small organization to put into practice without help. Draftera works in that gap.

02

Networks and associations

  • Training sessions for members, delivered to several organizations at once.
  • A plain-language AI policy template or short AI module for member resource libraries.
  • Group delivery of the full framework for a cohort of member organizations.

03

Funders

  • Capacity-building support for grantees, from a single session to the full framework for a cohort.
  • A summary of where participating grantees stand on AI governance, reported in aggregate.
  • Plain-language guidance for grantees on disclosing AI use in applications and reports.

04

Sector and standards bodies

  • A practitioner’s perspective on how national standards work in small organizations.
  • Plain-language implementation guidance that helps organizations meet existing standards.
  • Preparation of organizations for independent verification.

05

The framework behind the work

Draftera’s work is organized around Draftera Foundations, a framework for using AI well in nonprofits and social enterprises. It draws on the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, the OECD AI Principles and Canadian standards and guidance.
