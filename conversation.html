01

How it works

I learn how your organization works and where your team's time goes. Together we spot two or three places where AI could help. You leave with at least one idea you can put to use, whether or not we work together.

02

What we'll talk about

  • Where your team's time goes each week.
  • How staff are already using AI, formally or not.
  • One or two tasks that might be a good place to start.
  • Any worries about privacy, quality or staff reaction.

About the call

  • What you'll leave with

    At least one practical idea you can try with your team, even if we never work together.

  • What it isn't

    It isn't a sales pitch. I won't ask for your budget. If the Draftera AI Foundations Engagement or other support seems like a fit, I'll say so once. The decision is yours.

  • Who it's for

    Executive directors and operations leads at Canadian nonprofits, charities, social enterprises and associations. Board members are welcome too.

  • Before we meet

    Nothing to prepare. If you like, think of one task that takes more time than it should.
