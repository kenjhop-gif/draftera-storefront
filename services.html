Services and prices

Services

Three fixed-price ways to start, for nonprofits, social enterprises and the networks that support them

Each starter has a fixed price and a clear end point. Each one also puts part of Draftera Foundations, a framework for using AI in nonprofits and social enterprises, in place.

01

AI policy and staff session

$1,950

Leadership and policy · People and skills · Data and privacy

Who it is for:
An organization whose staff already use AI tools without shared rules, or whose board is asking about AI.
What you get:
  • A board-approved AI policy
  • A one-page guide for staff
  • Plain rules on what information stays out of AI tools
  • Wording for your board minutes
  • One round of revisions
  • A 90-minute session for your staff
How long it takes:
About three weeks from the first call to the staff session.
Also available:
A board and leadership session, a full data and privacy review, and a public transparency statement. Ask during your conversation. The data and privacy review is practical guidance, not legal advice.
What usually comes next:
A Workflow Build.

02

Group workshop

$1,500

Half-day $2,500

People and skills

Who it is for:
Networks, associations and funders who want to help several organizations at once.
What you get:
  • A planning call
  • A 90-minute session for up to 25 people, online or in person
  • A handout
  • A checklist each organization can use to see where it stands

A half-day version adds time for each organization to start its own policy.

How long it takes:
A planning call, then one session of 90 minutes or a half day.
What usually comes next:
Individual organizations start their own policy or Workflow Build, or the host arranges a program for a group of members. See the work for partners.

03

Workflow Build

$2,500

Two workflows $4,000

Value and measurement · Human oversight

Who it is for:
An organization with a recurring task that takes too much staff time, such as funder reports, intake records, meeting follow-up or donor thank-you letters.
What you get:
  • One working workflow, built with your staff on the tools you already have where possible
  • Staff training
  • A short written guide to what is permitted and who checks the output
  • A final report
How long it takes:
Two to three weeks.
What usually comes next:
The Embedded AI Partnership, for organizations that want to keep going.

04

Ongoing support

The Embedded AI Partnership is monthly support for organizations that have seen a workflow work and want more. It includes new workflows, staff training, a yearly check-up and help with operational questions.

05

How to start

The first step is a free 30-minute conversation. If working together makes sense, you receive a written proposal with a fixed price before any work begins.

Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes. Independent verification against CAN/DGSI 101, the National Standard of Canada for the ethical design and use of AI by small and medium organizations, is optional. It is carried out by the Digital Governance Council, which charges its own fee.
