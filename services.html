Services and prices

Services

Practical help with each piece of Draftera Foundations

Draftera Foundations is a framework for using AI well in nonprofits and social enterprises. Each service puts one piece of the framework in place and leaves your organization with a clear record of what was done. Start with the piece you need most, or build the whole framework.

01

Start with one piece

  • AI policy

    From $1,500

    Leadership and policy

    A board-approved AI policy and a one-page plain-language guide for staff, covering which tools are allowed, what they can be used for and what is off-limits. Developed with your executive director and reviewed with your board. You also receive wording for the board minutes that record the approval.

  • Training

    From $950 per session

    People and skills

    Information sessions and workshops for your board, leadership team or staff, each a tailored 90-minute session. The board and leadership session covers governing AI, the risks and the questions to ask. The staff session covers using AI safely under your policy and catching its mistakes. Each session includes a planning call, a handout and an attendance record.

  • Data and privacy review

    From $1,250

    Data and privacy

    A list of the AI tools your team actually uses and what information goes into them, clear rules on what stays out, and practical recommendations on tool settings. This is practical guidance, not legal advice; legal and cybersecurity questions are referred to the right professionals.

  • Workflow Build

    From $2,500

    Value and measurement · Human oversight

    One practical AI workflow for a real task, such as funder reports, donor acknowledgements or meeting notes, built with your staff over two to three weeks. It includes a named person who checks the output, a short how-to and a final report. Two workflows start from $4,000.

    More about Workflow Build →

  • Transparency statement

    From $350, as an add-on

    Transparency and trust

    A short public statement on how your organization uses AI, for your website, donor materials and grant applications. Available with any other service.

02

Build the whole framework

  • Full Foundations

    From $6,500

    Every piece of the framework, delivered in order. That means a data and privacy review, an AI policy, board and staff training, one Workflow Build and a transparency statement. It ends with a readiness summary showing where your organization stands against Canada’s national standard for AI in small and medium organizations, and it includes your first Foundations Check-up. Payment can be spread over stages or across two budget years.

  • Foundations Check-up

    From $1,500 per year

    A yearly review that keeps every piece current. It includes an updated list of the tools in use, a policy review against new laws and tools, a staff refresher and an updated readiness summary.

05

How to start

The first step is a free 30-minute conversation. If working together makes sense, you receive a written proposal with a fixed price before any work begins.

Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes, and depend on the size and needs of your organization. Independent verification against the national standard is optional and is carried out by the Digital Governance Council, which charges its own fee.
