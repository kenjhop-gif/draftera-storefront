How Draftera works

From expertise to an operating system.

Each engagement moves through the same four stages. This keeps the work grounded in the actual operation and makes the result easier to use, review, and improve.

01 / Diagnose

Understand the real work

Map the buyer, workflow, source material, constraints, and decisions that matter. Start with evidence, not a preferred tool.

02 / Design

Structure the method

Define the questions, evidence requirements, operating rules, deliverables, and human judgment points.

03 / Deliver

Put it into real use

Support implementation or the first live engagements, prepare working materials, and make each decision traceable.

04 / Govern

Improve without losing control

Track outcomes, overrides, effort, risks, and repeat use. Keep accountable people in charge of consequential decisions.

Evidence before assertion

Recommendations should show their work.

Draftera separates raw source, extracted evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and approval. That makes the work easier to review and keeps expert judgment visible.

Source

What was supplied

Documents, interviews, system records, and workflow observations.

Evidence

What is supported

Facts and patterns linked back to the original material.

Interpretation

What it means

Professional context, root-cause reasoning, and confidence.

Recommendation

What should happen

A practical next move with priorities and trade-offs.

Approval

Who owns the call

The accountable consultant or client decision-maker.

AI may prepare drafts. It does not approve its own conclusions.Draftera operating principle
