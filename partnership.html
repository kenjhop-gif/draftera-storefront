01

Why ongoing support

One workflow is a good start. Real change comes from steady work over months. New tasks come up. Staff change. Tools change.

Most nonprofits can't hire a full-time AI lead. The Draftera Embedded AI Partnership gives you that experience for a few days a month.

02

What I do as your embedded AI partner

  • Build new AI workflows as priorities come up.
  • Improve the ones already running.
  • Train new staff and refresh training for current staff.
  • Keep your AI guardrails current as tools and risks change.
  • Report hours and dollars saved, in a format you can share with your board.
  • Act as a sounding board for you on operations, staffing and change.
