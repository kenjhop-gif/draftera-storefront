01

Why ongoing support matters

A single workflow demonstrates what is possible, but lasting impact depends on ongoing stewardship. Priorities shift, teams evolve and the technology continues to advance, so your approach to AI needs to evolve with them.

Few nonprofits or social enterprises can justify a full-time AI lead, yet most need someone who knows their operations to keep the work moving. The Partnership provides that continuity for a predictable monthly fee.

02

What is included each month

Each month's time is directed to your organization's priorities, and every month covers the same core responsibilities:

  • New workflows

    I build new workflows as priorities emerge, starting with the tasks that take the most staff time.

  • Refinement

    I improve the workflows already in use, based on how staff actually work with them.

  • Training

    I train new staff and refresh training for current staff, so the tools stay in regular use rather than falling away.

  • Guardrails

    I keep your AI guidelines current as tools, risks and your own practices change.

  • Reporting

    I measure the hours and dollars saved and report them in a form you can share with your board.

  • Operational advice

    I am available as a sounding board on the staffing, process and change questions that AI work inevitably raises.

03

04

How a typical month works

The Partnership follows a consistent monthly rhythm, so your team always knows what is happening and what comes next.

  1. Review.We look at what has been built, how staff are using it and the time it has saved, and agree on the month's priorities.
  2. Build.I design and build new workflows, or improve existing ones, against those priorities.
  3. Train.I work with the staff who will use each workflow, so it becomes part of how they already work.
  4. Report.I summarize progress for your executive director, and each quarter prepare a report for leadership and the board.

05

Accountable for results

My involvement continues well beyond implementation. Each month, we review what has been built, how staff are using it and the time it has saved, refining what works and retiring what does not.

What leadership and the board receive

Every quarter, you receive a short impact report written for a board audience. It sets out:

  • the workflows in use and the teams using them;
  • the hours and dollars saved, and how they were measured;
  • any changes to your AI guardrails, and why;
  • the priorities proposed for the next quarter.

The report gives your board a clear, measured account of what the investment is returning, and gives you a straightforward basis for deciding whether to continue.

07

Who the Partnership is for

The Partnership is designed for Canadian nonprofits, charities, social enterprises and associations, typically with annual revenue of about $1 million to $20 million, that:

  • have seen what a working AI workflow can do, often through the Foundations Engagement, and want to extend it across the organization;
  • do not have, or cannot yet justify, a full-time staff member responsible for AI and operations;
  • want results they can measure and report to their board, rather than general experimentation;
  • are managing growth, restructuring or other change and want steady operational support alongside it.

If you have not yet started with AI, the Draftera AI Foundations Engagement is usually the better first step.

08

Common questions

Do we need to complete the Foundations Engagement first?

It is the usual starting point, because it gives us working workflows and a measured baseline to build on. If your organization already has AI work underway, we can discuss starting directly with the Partnership in the Introductory Conversation.

Which AI tools do you use?

I am tool-neutral. I work with the tools your organization already has where they are suitable, and recommend others only when there is a clear reason.

How do you handle our data?

Client, donor and staff personal information never goes into outside AI tools without your written agreement. Your guardrails set out what can be used, where and by whom.

Is the work done on site?

I am based in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, and work remotely with organizations across Canada, using video calls and your existing systems.

Can we move between Core and Plus?

Yes. If your needs change, we can adjust the level with 30 days' notice.

What happens after the first three months?

The Partnership continues month to month. Each quarterly report includes proposed priorities, which gives you a natural point to decide whether to continue, adjust the level or stop.
