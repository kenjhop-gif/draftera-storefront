The process

From expert practice to a product in use.

Draftera works alongside you to turn implicit expertise into a disciplined client experience. The method is tested before it meets a live client, then refined from real delivery.

01 / Extract

Your proven method

We study past reports, decks, questions, proposals, and delivery notes, then capture what you notice, what evidence you trust, and where professional judgment is required.

02 / Forge

A sellable diagnostic

We define the buyer, decision, scope, timeline, questions, evidence model, findings logic, and follow-on offer. Your expertise gains a clear commercial shape.

03 / Prepare

The client-ready package

Sales materials, questionnaires, interview guides, working documents, findings reports, and presentation templates are designed as one operating system.

04 / Deliver

The first live engagements

Draftera supports the setup, evidence work, draft findings, deliverables, and quality control. You retain the judgment, client conversation, and final approval.

The operating layer

Evidence before assertion.

The future workspace supports delivery. It does not replace the consultant. Its job is to keep the reasoning visible, connect findings to source material, and give the accountable expert the final say.

For the first three partners

Built with your practice, not for a demo.

The Founding Partner Program is for consultants who already have valuable work in the market and want to turn it into a more repeatable entry offer. It is not a course, a template library, or an outsourced sales agency.

A strong fit

  • You have paying clients and a proven advisory service.
  • You have past reports, proposals, or frameworks to work from.
  • You can identify a diagnostic stage in your current work.
  • You have a valuable follow-on engagement to sell.
  • You are willing to standardize part of the method.

Probably not yet

  • You are still trying to find your first consulting client.
  • You need Draftera to generate leads or close deals for you.
  • Your service has not yet been delivered more than once.
  • You want a generic platform to configure without expert input.
  • You need autonomous AI to make professional judgments.
