I ran a nonprofit. Now I help nonprofits make AI work.

I'm Ken Hopkins. I have spent my career in operations, first leading a growing nonprofit, then in senior university administration.

Running the School Lunch Association

From 2013 to 2019 I was Executive Director of the School Lunch Association in Newfoundland and Labrador.

We tripled revenue, from about $1M to $3.1M. We expanded to 34 school locations and served about 950,000 meals a year. I led a workforce of 85 people.

That meant long days, tight budgets and a team doing more than anyone asked. I learned that good systems protect good people.

Senior administration at Memorial University

Since 2019 I have been Senior Administration Officer for the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences at Memorial University. The faculty has 55 staff and a $31M+ operating budget.

My work covers workforce planning, budget reductions and employee relations. It is the practical side of making a large organization run. It also taught me how change lands with real people.

Draftera is my own independent practice. It is separate from my role at Memorial.

Why AI, and why nonprofits

I build working AI workflows myself. I have seen how much routine work they can take off a team.

I have also seen the gap. Staff are trying AI on their own, with no plan and no guardrails. Leaders are too busy to sort it out.

I started Draftera to close that gap for Canadian nonprofits, charities and associations. I bring an operator's view, not a vendor's. I measure results in hours and dollars.
